25 January · Farewell

The first look.

Subhana, mujhe aaj bhi hamara 10th class farewell yaad hai. Us din tumne white colour ka suit pehna tha aur black colour ke goggles lagaye the. Tumhari dance performance thi.

Jab maine tumhe pehli baar dekha, maine tum par zyada dhyaan nahi diya tha, lekin tum mujhe bahut achhi, cute aur pyari lagi thi. Mujhe tab bilkul idea nahi tha ki ye chhota sa moment meri story ka beginning ban jayega.

After Farewell · Instagram

Hi. Hello. You.

Farewell ke baad Instagram par hamare friends ka group tha. Wahi mujhe tum dobara dikhi aur maine tumse hi-hello se baat karna start ki.

Ek-do din baat karne ke baad maine Vishal ko bataya ki mujhe tum achhi lagti ho. Phir usne group mein ye baat bata di.

Tumne mujhse poocha ki tum mujhe achhi lagti ho, aur maine haan bol diya.

17 February

That “All the best.”

17 February ki subah tumne mujhe personally “All the best” bola tha.

Shayad tumhare liye woh ek simple message tha, lekin mere liye woh bahut special tha.

Usi din circumstances ke beech tumne mujhe haan kaha. Mujhe laga — shayad ye ladki mere liye hi bani hai.

4 March · Rose

The rose I still remember. 🌹

Jab tumne mujhe mana kar diya tha, uske baad 4 March ko main school gaya.

School ke saamne, bahut saare bachchon ke saamne maine tumhe rose diya.

Tumne mera rose accept nahi kiya. Lekin us moment ke baad bhi maine apni feelings ko chhod nahi diya.

18 March · New Beginning

We continued.

Phir 18 March ko tumne mujhe dobara haan kaha, aur uske baad hamari story continue hui.

Kuch din sab achha chala. Phir Eid par tumhari Appi ko hamare baare mein pata chala aur ek aur difficult phase aa gaya.

Main efforts karta raha, aur 4 April ko tumne mujhe phir haan kaha.

21 May · Another Chance

Another “yes”.

Phir 21 May ko tumne mujhe dobara haan kaha. Maine purani mistakes ko side mein rakhkar relationship ko genuinely aage badhana chaha.

Fights aur problems phir bhi aati rahi, lekin meri feelings aur efforts ruk nahi gaye.

9 June → July · Birthday

A gift kept waiting. 🎁

9 June ko tumhara birthday tha. Main tumhare liye gift lekar aaya tha, lekin birthday par hum mil nahi paaye.

Baad mein July mein jab hum mile, maine tumhe woh gifts diye. Uske baad tumne mujhe phir haan kaha.

Phir bhi journey mein fights aur misunderstandings aati rahi.

September · Exams

The moment everything felt real.

September mein exams ke time hum evening mein mile. Tumhare family ke ek relative ne humein saath dekh liya. Tumhare mummy-papa ne tumhe bahut daanta.

Woh moment bahut scary tha, lekin situation sambhal gayi. Mujhe laga us din uparwale ne humein bacha liya.

Everything between us

Not perfect. Still special.

Subhana, hamari journey mein bahut fights hui, misunderstandings hui, problems aayi aur bahut baar cheezein difficult hui.

Logon ne bhi mana kiya, aur hamare beech religion ka difference bhi ek reality hai.

Lekin mere liye hamari story sirf happy moments ki story nahi hai. Ye efforts, patience, feelings aur memories ki bhi story hai.

“Har easy moment yaad reh sakta hai, lekin difficult moments humein story ki value samjhate hain.”

17 October · Nine Months

Finally…

9

Months Complete 🤍

Subhana, finally aaj 17 October hai aur aaj apne 9 months complete kar liye hain. 🥹🤍

Aaj bohot special moment hai. Itne saare moments, fights, misunderstandings aur memories ke baad aaj bas is moment ko saath mein feel karte hain.

Main tumhe kabhi force nahi karna chahta. Chahe tum mujhe utna pyaar na kar pao jitna main tumse karta hoon, meri feelings tumhare liye fake nahi hain.

Relationship ho ya na ho, main tumhari respect aur tumhare liye apni care dil mein rakhunga.

Aise hi apne 7 saal tak hamesha saath rahenge. Promise. 🤍

2032 · The Dream

Seven years from now.

Subhana, mera dream hai ki ye 7 saal bhi ek din complete ho jaayein.

2032 mein main tumhare ghar tumhara haath maangne aaunga.

Phir agar hum dono tab bhi ek-doosre ko choose karna chahein, toh apna future saath build karenge.

Apna ghar hoga, hum duniya explore karenge, tumhare dreams ko support karenge aur ek beautiful life banane ki koshish karenge.

Future mein problems aayengi, mujhe pata hai. Main chahta hoon ki hum unhe patience, respect aur understanding ke saath face karein.
